# Contact delivery gate

Direction: match skills.html and experience.html. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Cream and burnt orange preserve the portfolio palette; Georgia preserves the heading voice; Arial supports form readability. The split layout separates the invitation from input, a single white panel groups the form, and smaller mobile gaps preserve reading order. Corners use 6px controls and a 12px panel. No imagery or decorative icons are needed.
